What This Site Is

The Bhairava Baba and Ma Adya Mahakali Upasana Calendar is a fully static website. There is no backend, no database, no user account, and no email collection. All calendar data is embedded in static files served directly to your browser.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or any personal information.
  • We do not use cookies or local storage.
  • The project itself does not include analytics or tracking scripts.
  • We do not use advertising networks.
  • Filters, event details, and .ics downloads all happen in your browser.

Hosting Infrastructure

This site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare may collect standard server-side access logs (IP address, browser user-agent, request timestamps) as part of its infrastructure operations. This is outside our control. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for details.

Third-Party Requests

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts. Those requests are made directly to Google and are subject to Google’s Privacy Policy. No calendar data or personal information is sent with those requests.

Calendar Export (.ics Files)

The “Export Calendar” feature generates a downloadable .ics file entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server during this process.

External Links

This site links to external sources, including NASA, Drik Panchang, and the Jai Khyapa Parampara website. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

Disclaimer

Date accuracy: All tithi dates use the Indian Standard Time (IST) sunrise rule — the standard method for panchanga calculation in India. If you are outside India, the correct observance date may fall one day earlier or later because the tithi prevailing at your local sunrise can differ from the one at sunrise in India. Always verify with a location-aware panchanga, such as Drik Panchang set to your city, before observing a vrata or utsava.

Eclipse data: Eclipse dates and greatest-eclipse times are derived from NASA’s eclipse catalogue. Dates are shown in IST; timings are shown in both IST and UTC. Extend the times to local circumstances before making travel or observance plans.
